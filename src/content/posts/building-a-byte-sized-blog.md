Getting started with a blog doesn’t have to be complicated. In fact, sometimes the best way to learn is by building something intentionally small. A byte-sized blog strips things down to just the essentials—render a page, display some content, and ship it. No overthinking, no overengineering, just momentum.

For this demo, the goal isn’t to create the perfect platform—it’s to create something functional and approachable. By focusing on a minimal design and a couple of posts, you can experiment freely and iterate quickly. Once the basics are in place, you can layer in more features over time, but the important part is getting something working fast.
