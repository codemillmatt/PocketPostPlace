description: "Of course they matter!"
pubDate: "2026-05-26T23:03:27.000Z"
---

There’s something powerful about working on small, contained projects. They remove friction and let you focus on learning instead of logistics. A simple blog, like the one you’re seeing here, is a great example—it’s just big enough to be interesting, but small enough to understand completely.

Small projects are also perfect for demos and talks. They’re easy to explain, quick to build, and flexible enough to adapt on the fly. Whether you’re exploring a new framework, testing an idea, or showing off a tool, starting small gives you the confidence to move fast and keep things fun.
